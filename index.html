Our story

Two street food traditions, one kitchen.

Masamesou started with a late-night question: what if the corn tortilla and the bowl of noodles shared a table? Our tortillas are pressed by hand every day, our broths simmer for hours, and our salsas borrow from both sides of the Pacific.

We cook with chilies and citrus from both traditions, buy from nearby growers, and keep the menu playful. Come for tacos, stay for ramen, and order a little of everything.
