What Is pluck?

pluck is a small program you install on your computer. Instead of typing complicated commands, you open the app, paste a link, and let it do the work. Under the hood it uses a well-known tool called yt-dlp, but you never have to touch that part yourself.

Think of it as a helper that sits on your desktop. You give it a link to a video or a song, and it saves a copy to your computer so you can watch or listen later, even without an internet connection.

Because pluck keeps its own tools up to date, you do not need to hunt for new versions every few weeks. It handles that for you quietly in the background.

🔄 Self-Updating Tools

Websites change all the time, and downloaders can stop working when they do. pluck avoids that problem by checking for and installing fresh versions of its own engine. You usually do not need to do anything at all.

If a download suddenly fails, the most helpful first step is simply to restart the app. On start-up it looks for updates and brings itself up to date, then you can try the link again.
